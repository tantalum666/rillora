## Design

Two unequal open pages form one compact emblem. It places more emphasis on organized information and a trusted next reader, with Inter Medium type and warm clay rather than the first design's letter monogram and serif type. This has a more literal book/folio association; it is a deliberate visual difference, not a claim of uniqueness or greater user preference.

Palette: clay `#AD5F43`, paper `#F6F1E8`, ink `#28322D`. These are Logo concept colours; no App palette was changed.

## Editable sources and provenance

The symbol comes from one new built-in ImageGen concept, followed by a requested opaque-background correction, then explicit Bézier reconstruction. It is not an automatic bitmap trace. The micro variant widens the gutter and separates the lower tips. At 16/24/32/64/128px, the generated symbol still has two separate visible pixel components.

The wordmark uses **Inter Medium with -3% tracking**, outlined in Figma. The live text reference remains editable as node53:3. `Inter-OFL.txt` is included. The comparison also uses Version 1's Source Serif 4 wordmark; its licence is included without changing or redistributing a modified font. No new paid font or third-party Logo was imported.
