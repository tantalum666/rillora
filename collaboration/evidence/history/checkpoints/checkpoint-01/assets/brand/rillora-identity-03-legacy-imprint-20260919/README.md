# Rillora — Legacy Imprint / identity03

Status: NEW DESIGN FOR REVIEW; IDENTITY01 RETAINED; IDENTITY02 REJECTED; PRODUCT UNCHANGED  
Date: 2026-09-19

Owner rejected the book/pages design because it had insufficient meaning and product relevance, and requested another version. This new symbol combines fingerprint-like personal traces with a central tree. The intended association is an individual's digital life continuing to trusted people. It is a visual metaphor, not a biometric feature, automatic release or inheritance guarantee.

## Design and sources

One new built-in ImageGen concept and one simplification study informed explicit vector reconstruction. The initial study was too detailed; the delivered symbol removes all leaf illustrations, regularizes broad curves, separates accidental joins and has a simpler 16/24px variant. The SVG uses editable paths and scalable strokes with round ends, not embedded bitmaps or automatic image tracing.

The Source Serif 4 SemiBold wordmark is reused from identity01 as a read-only source to keep the comparison focused on the symbol. Its original files were not altered; the existing OFL is included. The palette is forest #23473D, paper #F5F1E8 and bronze #BD9473. These are design colours, not an App-wide palette change.
