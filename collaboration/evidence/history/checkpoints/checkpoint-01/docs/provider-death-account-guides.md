## Findings That Change The Guidance

### Binance And Binance.US

The earlier generic-chat-only direction is superseded. Binance now documents a concrete flow: the applicant uses their own account, then Customer Support → Self Service → View All → Account → Legacy Inheritance. Identify the deceased account by email, phone or Binance ID and submit legally valid inheritance documents requested by Binance. Link the actual Inheritance Appeal form as well as the help article. This is an after-death claim, not a living-user Legacy Contact switch. Binance.US keeps its own material list; do not apply it globally.

- [Binance official inheritance steps](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/89413ec723d94a1d8fb038e3e1130e0a)
- [Binance Inheritance Appeal](https://www.binance.com/en/my/risk/appeal?templateId=T192d41590)
- [Binance.US deceased-account process](https://support.binance.us/en/articles/10395308-collecting-funds-from-a-deceased-user-s-account)

### Access, Cancellation And Transfer Are Different

Apple has a Legacy Contact route and a separate family/legal-document route when no key is available. Google warns to request desired content before closing the account. Dropbox may require a qualifying court order. Adobe and Netflix guidance chiefly addresses closure/cancellation. Steam's personal license rules do not establish an inheritance transfer service. Yahoo U.S. forms reject non-U.S. requests; regional support instructions remain distinct.

- [Apple setup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102631), [request without a key](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102431)
- [Google preparation](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3036546?hl=en), [deceased-account request](https://support.google.com/accounts/troubleshooter/6357590?hl=en)
- [Dropbox deceased-account guidance](https://help.dropbox.com/account-settings/access-account-of-someone-who-passed-away)
- [Adobe closure instructions](https://helpx.adobe.com/account/individual/subscriptions-and-plans/renewals-and-cancellations/close-account-behalf-someone-else.html)

## Safety, Marks And Maintenance

- A contact, an inactivity timer, a checked guide or a Rillora record does not prove death, inheritance, authority, access, deletion, transfer or ownership.
- Rillora supplies directions and official HTTPS links. It does not collect provider credentials, recovery secrets, payment credentials, identity/death documents or submit a claim. A step saying upload or submit always refers to the named provider's official process.
- Tests constrain links to explicit provider-owned hosts. Redirects, local availability and signed-in forms remain provider-controlled; a working help link is not evidence that an application will be accepted.
- Only the original five IDs support existing optional self-report records: apple, google, meta, github, password_manager. Splitting Instagram does not reassign Meta history or add readiness credit. New guides do not create backend rows or imply completed preparation.
- Source help pages do not license provider logos. Signed-in Web and contextual guidance share the adopted GitHub/X/Instagram/Facebook/Dropbox/Coinbase marks; the existing Apple native system symbol remains separate. Coinbase's official CDS geometry uses its source copyright license plus a separate bounded identifying-use assessment. - Re-open sources when changing content and before publication. If a source cannot be fully read, preserve its last verified date and record the exact limitation; do not treat a login page, HTTP200 or search snippet as full verification. Provider-specific freshness is stored per guide.
