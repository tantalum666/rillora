## Earlier exploration brief — historical

Rillora helps a person organize their digital life and share selected information with people they trust. The identity should feel considered, warm and durable, while remaining legible in an App icon and a small navigation mark. It need not illustrate death, explain every product function or prove legal authority.

Explore distinct constructions, then select the strongest as one coherent identity. This round starts with a flowing R monogram, an interleaved organic symbol and a compact folded form. These are working directions, not commitments to adopt all three. The full name must read accurately as Rillora. Colour and typography are design choices, not additional approval gates.

Useful checks remain practical evidence: clean contours, positive/reversed versions, small-size visibility, sensible icon padding, consistent symbol/wordmark proportion and source/licence provenance. Do not turn a subjective score into user research or call an AI bitmap a finished vector master.

## First design result

Agent selects **Flowing R** as the primary design. The R gives direct name recognition; the stable upper bowl and flowing lower leg provide one clear visual gesture. The botanical study was not developed because it reads strongly as a tulip/plant; the folded-loop study was not developed because its repeated cuts suggest recycling. These are visual judgments, not user-study results.

The R was rebuilt as a closed Bézier vector with an optical variant for 16/24px. A Source Serif 4 SemiBold wordmark preserves clear Rillora spelling. The palette is pine #183B31, warm paper #F5F1E8 and sage #CBDDAF. The delivered package includes outlined SVGs, transparent PNGs, positive/reversed artwork, horizontal lockups, website icons and 1024px opaque RGB App icon studies. The small-size proof and final Figma render were inspected; an initial inherited-colour mismatch and tight lockup canvas were corrected before final export.
