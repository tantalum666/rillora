## Locked Exploration Constraints

- Do not require an `R` lettermark.
- Do not literally illustrate death, inheritance, water, wood, a tree, leaf, wave, heart, hand, shield, lock, key, vault, fingerprint, sparkle or blockchain.
- Express one product-relevant action rather than diagramming the whole product.
- Judge black silhouette and small-size behavior before color, texture or mockups.
- Keep product meaning separate from legal claims: a mark may suggest continuity or handoff, but cannot imply guaranteed disclosure, ownership transfer or universal legal validity.

## 2026-08-07 Selected Aesthetic Coordinates

Owner selected `Apple / Mastercard / Chase / Polestar`. This is a calibration lock, not a request to blend their shapes or copy a protected mark.

### Shared visual character

- Each mark can stand independently without relying on a wordmark or explanatory copy.
- The footprint is compact and immediately scannable, with one dominant silhouette rather than several decorative gestures.
- Geometry is disciplined, but one structural event creates memory: a cut, overlap, rotational void or precise convergence.
- Positive mass and negative space are both designed; the empty area is structural rather than leftover background.
- Stability and tension coexist. The marks feel balanced without becoming static, soft, cute or wellness-like.
- They remain convincing in one colour. Colour strengthens recognition but does not rescue weak geometry.
- They do not attempt to diagram the whole company. Meaning accumulates through consistent use rather than a long Logo explanation.

The selection does **not** prove that Owner wants circles, strict symmetry, sharp corners or a literal object. Apple is pictorial and asymmetric, Mastercard is soft and overlapping, while Chase and Polestar are angular and constructed. Their useful intersection is precision, reduction and a single memorable structural move—not one common shape family.

### Next non-letter mark boundaries

1. The symbol contains no `R`, hidden letter, monogram or typographic fragment.
2. Use one to four primary masses only. Every mass must change the silhouette or the essential negative space; no decorative fragments.
3. Build around exactly one structural event: `cut`, `overlap`, `counter/void` or `convergence`. Do not combine several tricks in one mark.
4. The symbol must have a compact App-icon footprint and remain identifiable at `64 / 32 / 24 / 16px` in black, reverse and one-colour use.
5. Use flat colour only. First-round judgment is monochrome; later colour may use no more than two principal brand colours.
6. Prefer optical balance over perfect mathematical repetition. Any asymmetry must be deliberate and visible at normal size.
7. Keep emotional tone controlled, assured and durable: neither playful, cute, calligraphic, wellness-organic, luxury-fashion nor generic enterprise-tech.
8. Do not add literal water, wood, death, inheritance, document, door, portal, security or legal imagery. Rillora's meaning should not depend on an explanatory metaphor.
9. Explicit similarity exclusions: no bitten silhouette or detached leaf; no two overlapping circles; no four rotating wedges around an octagonal void; no mirrored four-point star or opposed diagonal lozenges.
10. The concept round must show each mark large, at 24px, reversed, as an App tile and beside the full `Rillora` name. No atmospheric mockups or large identity boards before the mark itself survives.

### Three allowed structural territories

- **Single mass / controlled cut:** one compact silhouette made memorable by one precise removal; the form must not resemble fruit, a bite or an initial.
- **Two masses / emergent space:** two unlike forms create a third, ownable negative space; circles and chain/link readings are prohibited.
- **Balanced convergence:** three or four limited elements create a stable centre without rotation, a star, aperture, sparkle or Chase-like octagon.

## 2026-08-08 Reset Round Rejection and AI-method Correction

Owner rejected all three reset marks and described them as child-like drawings. All three are closed: do not refine, trace, combine or reuse their silhouettes. The failure came from the production method as well as the drawings. Long prompts with many negative exclusions and four real trademark references left the raster model to fill an overconstrained residual space with generic blobs; asking it to deliver a finished mark also skipped controlled sketch variation, editable paths and optical correction.

Current official guidance and primary research support a hybrid process:

- OpenAI recommends short, clear prompts, a small manageable set of references and one targeted revision at a time rather than a long list of competing instructions.
- Midjourney likewise recommends short positive descriptions and warns that long detail lists reduce control; style references are for visual character, not a substitute for content structure.
- Adobe's professional Logo guidance keeps selected sketches black and white before vector development. Illustrator's current Concept to Vector workflow can use a sketch as the structural source and return editable vectors with controllable reference fidelity.
- Recraft's current Vector models can produce editable SVG alternatives, but model limitations and commercial claims do not remove the need to inspect and rebuild paths.
- ACCV 2024 research on vector Logo synthesis still reports unnecessary paths and difficulty controlling results entirely through text prompts.
- IBM maintains optically different positive and reversed masters, demonstrating that mathematical reuse alone is not production correction.
- The U.S. Copyright Office states that prompts alone do not supply sufficient human authorship; creative human selection, arrangement or modification may.

The proposed Rillora workflow is therefore `strategy -> text-only concept propositions -> simple proprietary skeleton sketches -> sketch-conditioned/native-vector AI variations -> manual vector reconstruction -> optical and similarity gates -> colour`. Raster image generation must not again be presented as a finished Logo candidate.

## 2026-08-08 Gate 0 — Text-only Concept Propositions

以下三份命题按`docs/logo-ai-workflow.md`的Gate 0生成。它们是概念策略证据，不是Logo草图或候选；结构骨架说明也只是下一门可能采用的构造假设。本门没有生成或展示图片。

### 1. The Deliberate Interval / 有意之隙

**One-sentence proposition:** Rillora preserves a deliberate interval between preparing private intent now and releasing the right information later, so care can continue without premature access.

- **核心动作：** 把“现在准备”与“条件满足后披露”隔开，并在验证正确角色与情形后才允许跨越这段间隔。
- **情感张力：** 亲密关怀与克制边界同时存在；不是冷硬封锁，也不是毫无防备的交付。
- **Rillora专属性：** 产品明确区分资料存在、合法授权、账号访问、信息披露与所有权转移，并按条件分阶段披露。普通密码库强调随时取用，普通SaaS强调即时流转，都不是这项动作。
- **最可能误读：** 安全软件、托管、保险箱、门或建筑入口。必须排除盾牌、锁、钥匙孔、门框、嵌套容器和开启箭头。
- **文字化骨架说明：** 一个紧凑的单一外部轮廓，由一处偏轴但受控的开放间隔组织；间隔只制造“尚未跨越”的结构张力，不形成门、孔洞或字母。整体接近方形但不机械对称，只有一个结构事件，没有附属碎片。

### 2. The Next Reader / 后来者可读

**One-sentence proposition:** Rillora turns a private and scattered life map into a calm next step for one future authorized reader, without asking that person to impersonate or replace the planner.

- **核心动作：** 把只有本人理解的分散事务，整理成另一位被授权者未来可以准确理解并合法行动的下一步。
- **情感张力：** 私密与可读、个人声音与他人行动、靠近与不越界同时成立。
- **Rillora专属性：** Rillora同时服务Planner与未来的Trusted Contact、Executor或Recipient；目标不是把凭证交给另一个人，而是让对方知道有什么、去哪里找、按什么身份处理。这种“为下一位读者准备”的双端关系不是普通文件、消息或密码产品的默认机制。
- **最可能误读：** 书页、信封、聊天气泡、眼睛、播放／下一步按钮。必须排除纸张折角、对话尾巴、睫毛、三角箭头和字面阅读符号。
- **文字化骨架说明：** 一个有明确阅读方向的紧凑单体，主体从较密的一侧经过一次精确的轮廓转折，抵达更清晰、开放的另一侧；不拆成两个角色头像，不画传递路径。比例略纵向，负空间只用于形成方向感，不形成页面或眼睛。

### 3. One Plan, Different Endings / 一体分续

**One-sentence proposition:** Rillora keeps one coherent plan while allowing each matter to continue differently—transfer, close, delete, memorialize, delay or remain undisclosed.

- **核心动作：** 把不同资产、负债、账号、设备和资料纳入同一计划，同时保留每一项不同的接收人、条件与处理结局。
- **情感张力：** 统一秩序与个体差异同时存在；整体让人安心，但不把复杂人生压成“全部交给一个人”的简单答案。
- **Rillora专属性：** 逐项表达转移、关闭、删除、纪念化、延迟公开或永不披露，是Rillora产品定义里的核心区别。通用项目管理或工作流产品通常追求所有事项进入同一种完成状态。
- **最可能误读：** 网络拓扑、物流分发、家族树、数据路由或进度图。必须排除节点连线、分叉树枝、中心枢纽、箭头、饼图与完成环。
- **文字化骨架说明：** 一个连续、稳定的外部质量承担整体识别，内部只通过三处不等量但同属一个秩序的边缘变化表达不同结局；变化沿同一侧依次发生，不从中心放射，也不形成树枝或多个零件。整体保持一体，差异来自节奏而不是碎片数量。

### Gate 0 internal assessment

- `2 — The Next Reader`最具Rillora专属性和人性：它直接连接Planner与未来执行者，同时天然包含“不冒充本人”的边界。
- `1 — The Deliberate Interval`最容易形成克制、成熟的结构张力，但必须严防安全／门户品类误读。
- `3 — One Plan, Different Endings`最完整地表达产品广度，但视觉上最容易滑向网络、分流或信息架构图。

设计侧推荐顺序为`2 > 1 > 3`。这只是Gate 0内部判断，不替代Owner选择。
