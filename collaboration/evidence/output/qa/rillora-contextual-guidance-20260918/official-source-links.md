## Important limits

- Apple and Binance were checked through the own-account login boundary. Instagram memorialization also requires login.
- Google data-request and Adobe/X initial forms were visibly reached; no fields, agreements or submission completed.
- Coinbase’s current unauthenticated Executor Services entry reached a generic selector and did not advance in this browser. Keep the official instructions and own-account prerequisite visible; do not claim an end-to-end pass.
- Microsoft, Dropbox, PayPal and several other providers use legal/support review; no universal automatic transfer is promised. Steam guidance is about license limitations, and Adobe/Netflix principally about cancellation.
- The automated public-link probe is transport evidence only. Scripts received403/202 on some sites whose actual browser pages worked. The browser observations above take precedence over interpreting those statuses as a broken process.
