This brief operates inside `docs/logo-ai-workflow.md`. It does not replace the locked hybrid-production contract. Its purpose is to prevent another technically valid but visually weak batch from reaching the Owner.

## 1. Creative Problem

Design a non-letter graphic mark for **Rillora**, a global digital-legacy and affairs-continuity product. The mark must feel calm, exact, self-possessed and durable without looking like legal software, cybersecurity, fintech, wellness, funeral services or a generic AI/SaaS app.

The mark is not required to diagram the product. `The Next Reader / 后来者可读` remains the strategic concept, but it is a meaning that the identity may accumulate; it is not a drawing instruction.

## 2. Brand Facts, Not Graphic Commands

- Rillora helps one person prepare information that another authorised person may later need to understand and act on.
- The experience should feel calm and responsible, not morbid, mystical or alarmist.
- The product distinguishes knowing, authority, access, disclosure and ownership rather than collapsing them into one promise.
- The name carries quiet continuity through `rill + flora`, but the mark must not literally draw water, leaves, trees or growth.

These facts guide tone and judgment. They do not prescribe two people, two fields, a path, a handoff diagram or an inheritance symbol.

## 3. Formal Target

The selected calibration marks — Apple, Mastercard, Chase and Polestar — are used only as evidence of formal discipline. Rillora should pursue:

- a compact silhouette identifiable without the name;
- one primary formal idea, plus at most one necessary secondary incident;
- controlled asymmetry or exact equilibrium rather than default symmetry;
- deliberate positive and negative space;
- black/white readiness and strong reverse behaviour;
- visual confidence at `16 / 24 / 32 / 64px`;
- a form that is simple to describe geometrically but not easy to confuse with a stock icon.

No fruit, overlapping-circle construction, rotating wedges, four-point star or other protected reference geometry may be copied.

## 4. Closed Topologies

The following are closed and must not return through renaming, interpolation or decoration:

- complete circle plus S-shaped boundary seam;
- taijitu, yin-yang fish, paired dots or traditional yin-yang proportions;
- standalone or hidden `R`;
- circle/squircle/hexagon used as a generic app-icon container;
- chain links, infinity loops, portals, shields, locks, keys or vaults;
- hands, people, hearts, eyes, speech bubbles, location pins, leaves, trees, waves or tombstones;
- radial three/four-part convergence around a central star or Y-shaped void;
- two rounded blocks creating a generic link or fintech mark;
- hard polygonal pieces that read as a flowchart, crypto token, developer tool or construction set;
- adding a small detached part whose only role is to make an ordinary shape look proprietary.

## 5. Five Genuinely Different Form Families

These are internal exploration territories, not five candidates and not five variations of one skeleton. Each family must have a different topology and perimeter logic.

### F1 — Singular silhouette

One continuous, asymmetric solid with a memorable perimeter event. No enclosed hole. The incident must change recognition of the entire silhouette, not resemble a bite, leaf, arrow or letter terminal.

### F2 — Designed counterform

One compact solid whose non-literal internal void is the identifying event. The exterior must not be a circle, squircle, shield or app tile; the counterform must not read as an eye, keyhole, letter or doorway.

### F3 — Unequal contact pair

Two unequal masses make exactly one optically controlled contact or near-contact. Their combined silhouette must be compact, while neither a chain link, overlap-circle nor two-person diagram appears.

### F4 — Primary form with structural witness

One dominant mass and one subordinate mass share a necessary alignment, cut or datum. Removing the smaller mass must destroy the formal idea; otherwise it is decoration and the sketch fails.

### F5 — Non-radial assembly

Three unequal necessary parts create one compact outer rhythm without enclosing a central star/Y void, forming an arrow, or resembling modular software/crypto geometry. This is the highest-risk family and should survive only with exceptional formal clarity.

## 6. Generation Discipline

- Produce a broad internal sketch field before selecting geometry; do not show the sketch field as a deliverable.
- Use one short positive prompt per family. Do not place the full exclusion list into a single generation prompt.
- AI output is reference material. It cannot pass because it satisfied the prompt.
- Programmatic geometry may test a chosen construction but cannot invent taste through parameter sweeps.
- Do not add a wordmark, colour, gradients, mockups, slogans or brand-story captions during blind screening.
- Do not attach concept names to the blind-review sheet; use random IDs so explanations cannot improve weak forms.

## 7. Hard-Fail Gate

A sketch is closed immediately if any one is true:

1. it resembles a letter, numeral or punctuation mark at first glance;
2. it falls into a closed topology above;
3. it needs a verbal explanation before it appears intentional;
4. it reads primarily as crypto, cybersecurity, legal, medical, wellness or developer tooling;
5. its recognisable feature disappears at `24px`;
6. it works only through colour, shading, outline weight or animation;
7. one component can be removed without weakening the core idea;
8. it produces an obvious first-glance resemblance to a known company mark;
9. its curves, joins or spacing appear accidental when enlarged;
10. multiple directions share the same topology with only parameter changes.

## 8. Blind Internal Score

Only sketches with no hard fail are scored. Maximum `100`; minimum submission threshold is `84`, with no category below its minimum.

| Criterion | Weight | Minimum | Question |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Silhouette and recall | 24 | 20 | Can the perimeter be distinguished and roughly recalled after a short view? |
| Formal coherence | 20 | 17 | Do proportions, curve tension, contacts and spacing feel deliberately resolved? |
| Structural necessity | 16 | 13 | Is every mass, cut and void necessary to the idea? |
| Small-size survival | 14 | 12 | Does the same identifying event survive at 16–64px and in reverse? |
| Category neutrality | 10 | 8 | Does it avoid default legal/security/fintech/AI readings? |
| Independent identity potential | 10 | 8 | Can it plausibly become recognisable without a copied reference geometry? |
| System fit | 6 | 5 | Can it sit beside `Rillora`, in an app icon and in document contexts without redesign? |

The score is an internal curation tool, not user research, legal clearance or Owner approval.

## 9. Submission Package

The Owner sees only forms that pass the hard-fail gate and score threshold. Each submitted form must be shown consistently as:

- one large black mark on white;
- one `64px`, one `32px`, one `24px` and one `16px` sample;
- one white-on-black reverse sample;
- one neutral pairing with the exact word `Rillora`, used only after the mark passes blind review;
- a short risk line stating the strongest likely misread;
- a clearly labelled evidence boundary: concept survivor, not production master or trademark clearance.

If fewer than two forms qualify, submit none and repeat internal exploration. A weak comparison set must not be padded to three.

## 10. Trademark Boundary

Broad topology crowding is screened before production investment. Candidate-level image and design-code searches begin only after a form passes the internal visual gate. Public USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO and national/regional searches are product-level pre-clearance, not a conclusion that a mark is registrable, usable or non-infringing.

- 针对性纠正要求单一实心封闭路径后，结果仍回归咬口水果／软团块、带游离小块的圆形、逗号／果实和聊天气泡；其中一枚还直接违反“单一路径、无分离部件”；

本实验零幸存者，因此没有诊断分、候选导出、人工重建或商标图形检索。它证明Figma Agent可以创建画板、布尔运算和可编辑矢量，但没有证明它能独立做品牌形态选择。后续只可在设计师先选定自有草图／骨架后，把它作为矢量构造或受控变体工具；不得把它继续用作下一枚Owner-facing起始形态的随机来源。
