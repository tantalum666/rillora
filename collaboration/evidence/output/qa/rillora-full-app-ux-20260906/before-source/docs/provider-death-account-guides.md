# Common Provider Death And Account-Continuity Guides

Role: focused official-source research and product-content contract  
Authority scope: common provider pre-death settings, deceased-account request routes, required inputs, official links and Rillora presentation boundaries  
Superseded by: newer provider policy, a later reviewed product decision, stricter applicable law or a verified regional process  
Do not use for: legal advice, proof of inheritance, permission to log in as another person, provider approval, transfer guarantee or current account-specific support outcome  
Status: OFFICIAL-SOURCE RESEARCH COMPLETE; IOS AND WEB GUIDE CATALOG IMPLEMENTED LOCALLY  
Last reviewed: 2026-08-20

## Why This Exists

Rillora promises that a future authorized person can find the provider's official next step. A generic checklist that only says `Apple / Google / GitHub reviewed` does not satisfy that promise. The product must provide:

1. a direct official source;
2. a short action the planner can take now;
3. a distinct route for a family member, executor or estate representative later;
4. the usual documents or account locators;
5. a clear statement of what the provider does **not** grant.

This research prioritizes globally common identity, cloud, social, developer, payment and custodial-crypto services with a publicly verifiable official route. It is a curated high-relevance catalog, not a numerical popularity ranking or exhaustive global registry.

## Critical Product Distinction

Provider mechanisms fall into two different classes and must not be presented as equivalents:

- **Prepare now**: the living account holder can choose contacts, data, recovery or a repository successor before an event. Apple Legacy Contact, Google Inactive Account Manager, Facebook Legacy Contact, GitHub successor and some password-manager recovery designs belong here.
- **Estate request**: the provider does not publish a comparable living-user legacy setting; after death, an authorized person submits a case, identity, death and authority evidence. Microsoft, LinkedIn, X, PayPal, Coinbase and Binance mainly belong here.

An inactivity timer is not a death determination. A legacy contact is not automatically an heir. A person able to help recover an account is not automatically entitled to its assets or contents.

## Platform Findings

### Apple

Planner action:

1. On iPhone or iPad, open `Settings -> name -> Sign-In & Security -> Legacy Contact`.
2. Add one or more contacts.
3. Share or print each contact's access key and confirm they received it.
4. Keep a second copy with estate-planning documents.

Later action:

1. Open Apple's Digital Legacy request site.
2. Submit the access key and death certificate.
3. Follow any country- or region-specific request for a court order or other legal document.

Apple states that a Legacy Contact may receive selected Apple Account data but cannot receive iCloud Keychain payment information, passwords or passkeys. Purchased movies, music, books and subscriptions do not transfer through Legacy Contact. Apple may limit the period during which approved legacy data remains accessible.

Official sources:

- [How to add a Legacy Contact](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102631)
- [Apple Digital Legacy request](https://digital-legacy.apple.com/)
- [Request access to a deceased family member's Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-mide/102431)

### Google

Planner action:

1. Open Inactive Account Manager.
2. Choose the inactivity period.
3. Select up to ten people and the specific data each may receive.
4. Choose whether Google should delete the account and keep contact details current.

Later action:

1. Open Google's deceased-user request flow.
2. Choose account closure, a funds request or a request for selected content.
3. Request content before closure when both may be needed; Google warns that closing the account prevents later content turnover.

Google evaluates activity using several account signals. That product mechanism is an inactivity plan, not death verification. Google states that it cannot provide passwords or other login details, and that every request is reviewed; providing content or funds is not guaranteed.

Official sources:

- [About Inactive Account Manager](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3036546?hl=en)
- [Open Inactive Account Manager](https://myaccount.google.com/inactive)
- [Submit a request regarding a deceased user's account](https://support.google.com/accounts/troubleshooter/6357590?hl=en)

### Facebook And Instagram

Planner action:

1. In Facebook Accounts Centre, open `Personal details -> Account ownership and control -> Memorialisation`.
2. Choose a Facebook Legacy Contact or Delete after death.
3. Decide whether the contact may download a copy of Facebook content.
4. Record an Instagram profile locator separately; no equivalent Instagram Legacy Contact setting was verified.

Later action:

- Facebook can memorialize or remove an account after a valid request.
- Instagram uses its own deceased-account memorialization/removal request.
- Removal may require proof of immediate-family relationship or estate authority in addition to proof of death.

A Facebook Legacy Contact cannot log in or read messages. Meta states that it does not provide another person's login information, and signing in as that person violates its policy.

Official sources:

- [Add, change or remove a Facebook Legacy Contact](https://www.facebook.com/help/1070665206293088)
- [Managing a deceased person's Facebook account](https://www.facebook.com/help/275013292838654)
- [Report a deceased person's Instagram account](https://www.facebook.com/help/264154560391256/)

### GitHub

Planner action:

1. Open GitHub `Settings -> Account -> Successor settings`.
2. Invite another GitHub user.
3. Confirm that the successor accepted.
4. For shared work, consider an organization with another owner rather than relying only on a personal account.

Later action:

1. The successor, next of kin or authorized person contacts GitHub Support.
2. Supply the deceased username, relationship, requested action and successor username if applicable.
3. Provide identity, death and authority evidence when GitHub requests it.

GitHub says a successor cannot log in as the deceased user. The documented successor scope covers archiving or transferring public user-owned repositories; it does not silently transfer private content, organization rights or account access.

Official sources:

- [Maintaining repository ownership continuity](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/repository-access-and-collaboration/maintaining-ownership-continuity-of-your-personal-accounts-repositories)
- [GitHub Deceased User Policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/other-site-policies/github-deceased-user-policy)

### Password Managers

Bitwarden provides Emergency Access. A grantor invites a Bitwarden user, the invitee accepts, and the grantor confirms; the grantor selects view or takeover access and a wait period. A later request can be approved or denied, or becomes available after the configured wait.

1Password's current family plan recommends at least one additional Family Organizer, saved Emergency Kits and recovery codes. Family organizers can recover another member's account; this is account recovery, not a provider death determination or proof of inheritance.

Rillora must never store the vault master password, 1Password Secret Key, recovery code or Bitwarden recovery material. These tools can expose extremely sensitive data and must be aligned with a valid estate plan and role model.

Official sources:

- [Bitwarden Emergency Access](https://bitwarden.com/help/emergency-access/)
- [Request and grant Bitwarden Emergency Access](https://bitwarden.com/en-gb/help/request-and-grant-emergency-access/)
- [1Password family recovery plan](https://support.1password.com/family-recovery-plan/)
- [Recover accounts for family members](https://support.1password.com/recovery/)

### Microsoft

Microsoft does not publish a general consumer Legacy Contact control. Its current deceased/incapacitated-account page says that Microsoft generally cannot disclose account information to non-account holders. In many regions, Microsoft must receive a valid subpoena or court order before considering release of Outlook, OneDrive or other Microsoft-account content. Germany and China have documented regional alternatives and required documents.

The planner should record the account identifier and subscription/payment locator without sharing the password. A later representative follows the page for the applicable region and separately stops recurring payments when account access is unavailable.

Official source:

- [Accessing Microsoft services when someone has died](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/account-billing/accessing-outlook-com-onedrive-and-other-microsoft-services-when-someone-has-died-ebbd2860-917e-4b39-9913-212362da6b2f)

### LinkedIn

Anyone with the requested identifying information may report a deceased member for memorialization. Closing the account requires an authorized person and LinkedIn-listed court documents. LinkedIn lists the member's name, profile URL, relationship, email, date of death and obituary/news link among the request inputs; accepted authority for closure includes court-issued Letters of Administration, Testamentary, Representation or another appointing court order.

LinkedIn states that it never discloses usernames or passwords. Memorialization locks account access, expires sessions and removes several active-network behaviors.

Official source:

- [Memorialize or close a deceased member's account](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1336663/memorialize-or-close-the-account-of-a-deceased-member?lang=en)

### X

X's published deceased-user route works with an authorized estate representative or a verified immediate family member to deactivate an account. X then requests further information by email, including requestor identity and the death certificate. It explicitly states that no relationship allows it to provide account access.

Official source:

- [Contact X about a deceased family member's account](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/contact-x-about-a-deceased-family-members-account)

### PayPal

PayPal's process is entity- and country-specific. The current US help page accepts instructions only from the authorized executor or administrator. It lists a cover letter identifying the account, requestor contact, death certificate, government ID and legal documentation identifying the executor. Regional pages may also ask how remaining funds should be handled.

The planner should record the primary PayPal email and exact PayPal country/entity, not a password or payment credential.

Official source:

- [PayPal US deceased-relative account closure](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/how-do-i-close-the-paypal-account-of-a-deceased-relative-help220)

### Coinbase

Coinbase's current guide states that an individual account cannot directly name a beneficiary. Estate planning documents or applicable intestate law determine ownership. The claimant signs in to or creates their own Coinbase account, uses Executor Services, and provides the death certificate, probate/estate documents, claimant photo ID and signed transfer instructions.

Coinbase—not Rillora—reviews the request and transfers custodial assets. This process is different from a self-custody wallet and must never be used as a reason to store a seed phrase in Rillora.

Official source:

- [Claim a decedent's Coinbase account](https://help.coinbase.com/en/coinbase/managing-my-account/other/how-do-i-gain-access-to-a-deceased-family-members-coinbase-account)

### Binance

This catalog corrects a potentially misleading product assumption: the current research did **not** verify a public global Binance feature equivalent to Apple Legacy Contact or Google Inactive Account Manager.

What can be verified:

- Binance terms and support materials recognize an inheritance application by an estate representative or authorized beneficiary.
- Requirements vary by Binance legal entity and may change.
- Binance.US currently publishes a dedicated guide requiring a selfie video, death certificate, claimant government ID and will/probate documents.
- The global Binance Academy article recommends that an executor contact the exchange with proof of death and authority, but some versions disclose that the article is contributor material; it is supporting context, not the primary user-action link.

Rillora therefore tells the user to record the exact entity—Binance.com, Binance.US or another regional entity—and open official support for that entity. It does not promise account access, quote one country's documents as globally sufficient or conflate custodial exchange assets with self-custody keys.

Official sources:

- [Binance official support chat](https://www.binance.com/en/chat)
- [Binance.US: Collecting funds from a deceased user's account](https://support.binance.us/en/articles/10395308-collecting-funds-from-a-deceased-user-s-account)
- [Binance.US Terms of Use](https://www.binance.us/terms-of-use)

## Providers Not Yet In The First Catalog

This review also looked for stable public deceased-account routes from widely used mainland-China platforms. The official Alipay service hall exposes general account support and the official hotline, but this run did not find a stable, public, deceased-account-specific instruction page suitable for a durable deep link. The official-search run likewise did not produce a sufficiently specific public WeChat/WeChat Pay page.

This does **not** mean those providers lack an internal estate process. It means Rillora must not invent documents, steps or contact routes from unofficial articles. Alipay, WeChat/WeChat Pay, banks, brokerages, gaming services, additional exchanges and regional fintech providers enter the catalog only after a provider-owned public source or verified support process is available for the intended market.

## Product And Security Contract

- The root page stays compact: provider, one-line purpose and reviewed state. Full steps appear only after opening a provider.
- Every visible link uses HTTPS and an explicit allowlist of provider-owned domains. Redirects and regionalization remain provider-controlled.
- Rillora opens the provider's page in the system browser. It does not embed a credential form, collect a provider password, upload death/identity documents or act as the applicant.
- Planner status remains `self_reported`. A checkmark means only that the planner says they reviewed the current official process.
- The app separates `Do now`, `If someone handles it later`, `Usually requested`, official links and the provider-specific boundary.
- A detail page hides the native Tab Bar so its official-link targets cannot sit behind system chrome.
- At accessibility text sizes, root actions and link rows reflow vertically and the page remains scrollable; text is not capped.
- Each guide displays the official-source review date and warns that providers can change requirements.
- A screenshot, Rillora record, legacy contact, recovery helper or provider request does not prove inheritance, consent, authority, access, deletion, transfer or ownership.

## Provider Mark Contract

- Official process links and official visual marks are separate permissions. A provider-owned help page does not automatically license its logo for a third-party directory.
- Rillora uses a provider's official downloadable mark only when the current published rules clearly cover this referential placement, the asset is preserved without recoloring／redrawing and the row cannot imply provider endorsement.
- GitHub and X currently use the exact official white assets on the shared neutral action-icon plate. Apple uses the system-supplied Apple symbol. The catalog does not tint any official mark sage.
- Google Identity's current `G` package is retained only for an actual Google sign-in button and is not reused as a generic guide logo. Microsoft, LinkedIn, Bitwarden and 1Password restrictions do not currently authorize the guide-directory placement; aggregated Facebook／Instagram and password-manager rows also cannot truthfully use one provider mark. These rows use neutral SF Symbol／Phosphor semantics rather than a fabricated logo.
- Source URLs, applicable restrictions and SHA-256 values are recorded in `docs/asset-licenses.md`. Recheck them with the provider-policy maintenance cadence.

## Evidence Boundary

The official links and summarized policies were reviewed on 2026-08-20. Local iOS and Web catalogs now cover eleven guide categories. This is product-content and local implementation evidence only. It does not prove every regional variant, live account eligibility, provider acceptance, legal sufficiency of a document, completed provider setup, family understanding, a successful estate request, asset transfer or external-user usability.

Provider policy is temporally unstable. Before publication and on a scheduled maintenance cadence, re-open every link, compare the live requirements with this contract, update the review date and preserve the prior wording as historical evidence rather than silently changing legal-effect claims.
