Use case: logo-brand. Design one original standalone symbol for Rillora, a calm premium consumer app that organizes someone's important digital-life information so selected trusted people can understand it. New direction: a compact open folio formed by TWO broad, softly folded, unequal pages. Let their outer curves gently hold a crisp narrow open central space, giving the feeling of care, openness and continuity. The left page is slightly lower and quieter; the right page rises with one graceful curved shoulder. The two shapes should feel like one memorable emblem, not a detailed book illustration. A strong, poised silhouette with substantial filled shapes and few deliberate Bézier-like curves; legible at small app-icon sizes. Render just ONE flat pure black symbol centered on a solid opaque white square canvas, generous margins. No words or letters, no circle enclosure, no gradients, no texture, no shadows, no perspective, no mockups and no alternate designs on the same image. This is a fresh second identity direction, not an R monogram and not a flower.
