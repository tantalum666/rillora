Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE original, memorable symbol for Rillora, a premium consumer service for organizing a person's digital legacy and passing selected information to trusted family or friends. The core idea is a personal imprint that continues: elegantly merge a simplified fingerprint and a small living tree into ONE coherent compact emblem. Use only a few broad smooth fingerprint ridges that grow from a clear central trunk; the same curves should read as personal identity and branching continuity, not two icons pasted together. The silhouette should feel calm, protective and human, with precise proportions and enough open space to work as a small app icon. Deliberate, strong filled or broad monoline geometry; no fine fingerprint texture. Draw it in a single very dark forest ink on a genuinely solid opaque warm-white background. Center one symbol with generous margins. No letters, words, labels, mockups, presentation boards, gradients, shadows or extra variants. No stock heart, shield or lock. This is about a person's life and their connections, not biometric authentication or a gardening business.
