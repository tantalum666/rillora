Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original Rillora logo symbol concept, square canvas.
Primary request: Create ONE distinctive capital R symbol from a continuous broad ribbon. It should feel warm, trustworthy, quietly premium and contemporary, suitable for a consumer app that helps people organize the digital life they want loved ones to understand.
Subject: A compact, confident R silhouette, optically balanced, with one elegant open counter and a leg that gently flows forward. Build the character through its contour and negative space, with substantial weight that remains legible at small sizes. The ribbon construction is structural, without rendering folds or depth. Avoid aggressive fintech styling and delicate fashion flourishes.
Style: Flat, crisp, vector-like black silhouette only, with considered curves and few purposeful gestures. No hairlines.
Composition: Exactly one symbol, centered on a solid opaque pure white square background, occupying roughly 55 percent of canvas height with ample whitespace.
Colors: Pure black mark, pure white background.
Text: Only the capital R itself; no wordmark, captions, slogans, presentation labels or extra lettering.
Constraints: Original symbol, no imitation of an existing logo, no multiple variants, no collage, no gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no texture, no mockup, no border, no watermark.
