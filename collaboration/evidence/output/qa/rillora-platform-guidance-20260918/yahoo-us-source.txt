Options available if a Yahoo account owner passes away
We know that dealing with the loss of a loved one is very difficult. Yahoo has processes in place to request the closure of the deceased user's account, to request the suspension of billing and premium services, and in certain circumstances to request content of the account. Any decision regarding a request will be made only after a careful review.
Unfortunately, we cannot provide passwords or other login details. To protect the privacy of the deceased user, it is our policy to honor the initial agreement that they made with us, even in the event of their passing. At the time of registration, all account holders agreed to the
Terms of Service (TOS)
. Pursuant to these Terms, all Yahoo accounts are non-transferable, even when the account owner is deceased.
Note: This help page applies to U.S. accounts only. Requests submitted for non-U.S. accounts will not be accepted and will not receive a response.
Requesting to close a Yahoo account
To process this kind of request, Yahoo requires specific documentation:
A copy of the death certificate of the Yahoo account holder, issued in the United States.
You can request the closure of the account through this
form
.
Requesting to suspend or cancel billing and premium services
To process this kind of request, Yahoo requires specific documentation:
A copy of the death certificate of the Yahoo account holder, issued in the United States. If a death certificate is not available, please contact Yahoo Customer Service at 866-265-4025.
You can request the suspension or cancellation of billing and premium services through this
form
.
Requesting content
To process this kind of request, Yahoo requires specific documentation:
A copy of the death certificate of the Yahoo account holder, issued in the United States; and
A copy of the requester's government-issued ID; and
A court order issued in the United States that satisfies Yahoo's requirements. Yahoo will provide you the required language for the court order.
You can request the content of the account through this
form
.
