Thank you. Please continue reviewing our eu-central-1 production-access request. Rillora is a pre-launch digital legacy organiser, not a financial custodian or a service that transfers funds. Accounts and subscriptions remain closed while release checks are completed.

Initial email use
We will initially use SES through Auth0 only for user-requested email verification and password resets. Password-reset mail applies to password-based accounts; social accounts use their provider's recovery. We will not send campaigns, purchased-list mail, cold outreach, account balances, private planning content or attachments. Contact invitation links are shared by the account owner; we do not automatically email their contacts.

Recipients and volume
The recipient address is entered in the user-initiated signup or recovery flow. There is no uploaded/imported mailing list. Our initial planning assumption is fewer than 100 active accounts and 0–20 transactional messages per day, not an existing customer count. We can retain the current 200/day and 1/second limits initially; the request is for external-recipient access, not bulk volume. The application will not schedule repeated verification or recovery reminders. We will complete tests with mailboxes we control before enabling the provider for customers.

Sending identity and failure handling
The sender will be Rillora <[REDACTED_EMAIL]>. SES currently reports SUCCESS for the domain, 2048-bit Easy DKIM and custom MAIL FROM bounce.notify.rillora.xyz. The sending subdomain has DMARC; the configuration set requires TLS and rejects a missing MAIL FROM MX instead of silently falling back.

The configuration set enables SES account-level suppression for BOUNCE and COMPLAINT. We will not remove a suppressed address to force delivery. CloudWatch receives aggregate delivery/failure events, with alarms for any bounce, complaint, reject or delivery delay routed to our existing owner alert subscription. A real mailbox-simulator check returned Send=3, Delivery=2, Bounce=1 and Complaint=1, and the alert actions executed. These are simulator results, not claimed real-user inbox delivery. We do not publish open/click tracking events or customer email bodies to logs.

Unsubscribe requests
There is no marketing subscription list or recurring campaign. These two message types occur only in response to an account action. People can ignore an unexpected verification/reset message, contact support@rillora.xyz, or request account deletion. Any future optional notification would require its own preference/opt-out implementation before activation. We do not claim that stopping optional mail disables necessary account-security handling.
