## Brand Name — LOCKED

The product name is **Rillora**. Use that exact capitalization in Web, iOS, product metadata and future brand exploration. `Arvemere`, `Orenmere` and all earlier names are research history, not alternate active labels.

The current concept image predates the selected name, so it cannot prove a final wordmark or Logo. Chinese naming, tagline, symbol geometry, wordmark typography and 24px identity behavior remain open visual gates.

## Logo Exploration Gate — LOCKED PROCESS

The final Logo is not selected. Owner rejected the initial generated directions as insufficiently premium, explicitly rejected the standalone English-letter `R` route, and then rejected both the Figma geometric round and the free-form image-generated symbol round because the specific designs were ugly. The rejection does not close the graphic-mark category.

- The Logo is an identifier, not a diagram of every product promise. Water, wood, continuity and trusted handoff may inform the design territory, but none must appear literally.
- Non-letter graphic marks remain valid. The next round must develop each graphic mark together with the full `Rillora` name and its real product applications; neither an isolated symbol nor a font-only wordmark counts as a complete direction.
- Do not judge a mark from a styled presentation board. First compare small black silhouettes, then pair surviving marks with the `Rillora` wordmark and test real Web/iOS contexts.
- “Premium” must come from an ownable visual move, proportion, spacing, optical correction and consistent application—not from a generic luxury serif, mockup, embossing, foil or explanatory story.
- Do not repeat quantity-led symbol matrices or isolated ImageGen marks. Compare a small number of genuinely different identity systems—non-letter graphic mark, exact full-name wordmark and real Web/iOS/document applications—then refine only a visually credible direction.
- Candidate checks must include 16/24/32/64px, reversed and single-colour use, silhouette recall, likely-category confusion, app-icon masking and preliminary visual-similarity screening.
- Generated images are ideation evidence only. They cannot establish final geometry, typography, small-size behavior, uniqueness, trademark clearance or Owner approval.

Discovery execution is recorded in `docs/logo-discovery.md` and the editable [Rillora Logo Discovery Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/xvtskr8gtVJWubDyDQfRUu). The visual crowding map, 40-thumbnail matrix, `Relay Seam / Proof Trace / Open Threshold` round and the later three free-form raster symbols are all rejected evidence. None is an approved final Logo, production asset or trademark-cleared mark. Current case research supports evaluating a complete identity system rather than isolated marks; it does not support abandoning graphic marks or forcing a wordmark-only solution.

The next complete-system round produced three raster identity boards with non-letter marks, full-name lockups and Web/iOS/document applications; Owner rejected all three. They are deprecated concept evidence only. Owner then selected `Apple / Mastercard / Chase / Polestar` as aesthetic coordinates. This locks a preference for compact independent silhouettes, disciplined geometry, structural negative space and restrained tension; it does not authorize copying those marks. The next non-letter mark boundaries and three structural territories are recorded in `docs/logo-discovery.md` and await Owner confirmation.

## Experience Character — LOCKED

- Calm, human and practical rather than morbid, legalistic or vault-like.
- Every app-owned surface uses matte graphite, limestone text, eucalyptus structure and one rust-clay primary action. Public discovery may explain one extra layer, but it uses the same compact cards, icon blocks, type scale and action grammar as authenticated tools; signing in must not feel like entering a different product.
- Editorial serif display type paired with a highly readable sans-serif UI face.
- Spacious rhythm, thin dividers, disciplined rectangular structure and almost no decorative effects.
- The interface should feel like a composed household plan, not an insurance funnel, crypto wallet or generic SaaS dashboard.

## Authenticated Root Hierarchy — OWNER-DIRECTED

The authenticated roots must be faster to scan than the public product explanation. Signing in means the user has already chosen the private-product context; Overview, Matters and Profile must not repeat the same empty fact in multiple explanatory cards.

- Empty Overview uses one short serif title, one primary Matter action and one visual progress surface. Progress, Matter count and trusted-contact status share that surface; do not render separate prose-heavy empty cards for each value.
- The progress surface uses a native circular completion indicator plus two tappable status modules. Matter opens `Matters`; trusted-contact status opens `Profile`. The indicator may animate only when progress changes and must stop animating under Reduce Motion.
- A populated Overview may show at most the first three current Matters plus `View all`; it does not repeat a full contact card or long legal boundary. The compact `Directions, not passwords` label remains, while detailed restrictions stay in the relevant editor or Safety destination.
- When first use is still available, Overview shows two visual choices—`Add a matter` and the optional guide—without repeating the same Add action in the hero. `Not now` remains reachable, and one short line explains that progress starts only after saving a Matter.
- Empty Matters does not show empty `Added` and `Needs review` sections. It uses one SF Symbol, `No matters yet`, one short category line and one primary Add action. Populated Matters retains native sections and item rows.
- Authenticated Profile uses one dedicated action surface and icon treatment for every task. Read-only technical values and actions must not be mixed in one card; internal `policyVersion` is not a root-profile user task.
- `Plan & access` compresses continuity state, authentication state and current plan label into one action row. Trusted-contact creation uses the short boundary `Record only · sends nothing`; the longer authority explanation belongs after entering the contact task.
- `Why I’m preparing` and optional setup are compact plan-detail actions. Preparation and Rillora support destinations use two-column SF Symbol tiles at ordinary text sizes and one column at accessibility sizes. Tiles use the same action surface, stroke, icon block and arrow grammar.
- Session actions keep the same card surface but use clay destructive labels. No root section uses a one-off green／black background that makes an action look like a different component family.
- Dynamic Type must reflow action rows vertically before any word fragments. Badges move beside the icon, titles receive full row width, and tool grids become one column; text remains uncapped and the page remains scrollable.

No gradients, glass, glow, faux vault doors or decorative security imagery. Raised panels use thin dividers and small tonal shifts instead of white cards or heavy shadow. System icons come from the platform/library rather than hand-drawn approximations.

Clay actions use graphite `canvas` text, not limestone. The measured relationship is approximately `4.89:1`; limestone on clay was approximately `2.99:1` and is not an accepted normal-text treatment.

## Typography — LOCKED RELATIONSHIP

- Display: Source Serif 4 or the closest commercially usable editorial serif.
- Interface/body: Inter or the platform-native sans where that improves platform behavior.

## Web Composition

- Web defaults to the global graphite token set for both public and authenticated routes. Workspace density and route hierarchy may change after authentication; canvas, surface, type and action grammar do not switch to a light product.
- The unauthenticated root is a full public introduction, not a centered sign-in card. It explains the product promise, three-step workflow, separate life-event paths and map-not-password boundary before asking for an account.
- `Account` is a distinct `/account` surface. Public calls to action move there first; only its neutral `Sign in` and `Create account` choices enter Auth0. It does not turn Free/no-card reassurance into a heading, plan card or action label. Private plan reads and every write remain authenticated.
- Public Web uses one compact hero and the same 16px tonal card／icon grammar as the authenticated workspace. Its product map is three static, scan-first rows—`What exists / What happens / Who can help`—rather than an expandable article index. The event boundary is three short visual facts, followed by one `Create my private map` action. `Account` remains a quiet header utility; public surfaces contain no plan comparison or price. A tall viewport may reveal the creation action only after the product map and separate-event facts have already appeared; this supersedes the older requirement that the action must always sit below the first physical viewport while preserving product-before-account order.
- Fixed left navigation on wide screens; overview content remains the dominant surface.
- Main content uses a generous reading column with the trusted-contact summary beside the matters list.
- The progress bar is five discrete segments, not a percentage chart.
- Responsive Web uses three explicit navigation states: the full 236px rail on wide screens, an 86px icon rail through compact desktop/tablet widths, and an opaque bottom navigation at 760px and below.
- Desktop Web keeps its complete workspace rail. Phone-width Web has exactly three primary destinations—`Overview / Matters / More`—with visible icon-and-text labels. `My plan`, trusted contacts, official guides, activity and settings remain reachable as secondary rows inside `More`; they are not squeezed into a horizontally scrolling seven-item tab bar.
- Mobile navigation targets remain at least 44px wide and 48px high and must not create document-level horizontal overflow.
- Keyboard focus, hover and selected states must be visible without changing the calm visual language.

## Interaction Contract

The first build must make visible core controls real:

0. Public visitors can understand the product without an account. Every iOS private/sign-in intent enters hosted authentication directly. Its required legal receipt and the server-owned session contract remain mandatory; no native intermediary or plan/price promotion is inserted.
1. After account creation or sign-in, the private overview is usable immediately. A questionnaire, Quick Win or setup modal cannot block navigation or manual matter creation.
2. First use offers two explicit paths: `Add your first matter` as the primary action and `Use the 2-minute guide` as an optional secondary action. Guided setup always exposes skip, save-and-exit, progress and resume. Web implements this as an Overview panel; iOS implements the guide as a native sheet. Both persist the shared state-step pair and preserve a minimum 44px/44pt action target. No preview path, fake Matter or prefilled demonstration draft is shipped.
3. `Continue your plan` opens or advances the next incomplete matter.
4. Matter rows open an editable matter detail.
5. Saving an incomplete matter updates the progress state.
6. Official provider guidance uses a compact root list and a focused detail page. Each detail separates `Do now`, `If someone handles it later`, `Usually requested`, official HTTPS links and the provider-specific limitation. Opening a link uses the system browser; the detail hides the Tab Bar so system chrome cannot cover the action. Only the existing planner checklist can change, and only as a clearly labelled self-report; no toggle claims to change or verify a provider.
7. Trusted-contact management shows record, role and invitation evidence separately; record edits do not claim to send an invitation.
8. Navigation and bottom tabs change the active product surface.
9. All simulations are explicitly labeled and never send real notifications or release real data.
10. Start Here preview states whether a file exists; the current no-file preview cannot expose sharing or download actions.
11. `Plan & access` is not promoted in public discovery. It is reached only after explicit plan-review intent inside the authenticated workspace or a named paid-capability intent. It presents Free as useful, Family as recurring coordination and any current billing-closed state explicitly; it never impersonates a checkout or client-side authorization result.

## Commercial Presentation

- Use one centralized comparison surface and contextual capability prompts; do not decorate every feature with a commercial badge.
- Free and Family share the same calm hierarchy. Family may receive a subtle surface distinction, but neither tier uses crowns, gems, trophy imagery or security-lock fear as a conversion device.
- A plan-price card shows the full monthly or annual charge, its billing period and an exact annual saving. A global research preview names the currency explicitly (currently `US$`); a live storefront must render its actual localized currency. Monthly and annual never look like different entitlement tiers.
- `Free`, `Family`, `preview`, `billing closed`, `grace` and `expired` are textual states, never color-only meanings.
- The UI differentiates `Continuity status`, `Authentication` and `Plan & access`; the word `Active` is not used without naming which state is active.
- Safe-downgrade copy stays close to the comparison or purchase decision, while baseline actions remain visually enabled and ordinary.
- A disabled or unavailable Family action must preserve a readable explanation and a minimum 44px/44pt target for the route that explains access.
