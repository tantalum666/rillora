## Factual Boundaries Preserved In Copy

The draft reflects the current product rather than a hoped-for production state:

- Rillora is a digital legacy and estate-continuity map, not a password vault, will, law firm, court, executor, bank or automatic transfer mechanism;
- passwords, full recovery codes, private keys, seed phrases and direct-transfer secrets are prohibited;
- Auth0/Google/Apple authentication is separated from the Rillora product database; the product database stores the identity issuer and subject and does not persist provider access or refresh tokens;
- Google and Apple sign-in currently request only basic identity information and do not request Gmail, Drive, Contacts, iCloud content or Apple Health data;
- current progress events are first-party, server-generated and privacy-filtered; there is no third-party advertising or analytics SDK;
- the 90-day product-event `expires_at` value is disclosed as a deadline marker, not proof of automatic deletion;
- current AWS Frankfurt logs use a 30-day retention configuration, without turning this into a global production-residency claim;
- real invitations, death claims, document verification and post-event release are not active;
- death, incapacity and being unreachable remain separate and no silence, missed sign-in, payment state, wearable signal, obituary or unilateral contact statement automatically releases information;
- current billing is inactive and future purchase terms must be shown before charging;
- no global legality, universal will validity, complete end-to-end encryption, independent production audit or guaranteed third-party action is claimed.
