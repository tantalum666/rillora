Description:

Rillora helps you leave less for the people you choose to figure out.

Build a private map of important accounts, documents, devices, ongoing bills and other matters. Record what exists, where the right information can be found and what you would like someone to do next.

ORGANIZE PRACTICAL DIRECTIONS

Add matters at your own pace. Keep useful locations, account hints and notes together, mark what needs review and update your directions as life changes.

CHOOSE WHAT EACH PERSON CAN SEE

Share selected matters with a person you trust. Choose whether they can see the name, practical directions or saved details. Review that view before sharing, and change or revoke access when needed. Recipients can use a secure web entry without installing the iPhone app.

KEEP A COPY AND STAY IN CONTROL

Preview a Start Here guide for yourself or a chosen person. Export the supported copies, back up your map, and manage your account data from the app. Copies already saved by another person cannot be remotely erased.

FREE AND FAMILY

Free includes one outgoing live sharing connection. The optional annual Family subscription supports up to five and adds the ongoing collaboration features shown in the app. The current price, renewal period and cancellation information are shown before you confirm a purchase. Your existing paid period and historical purchases remain subject to their original terms.

DIRECTIONS, NOT PASSWORDS

Rillora is a planning and information-sharing tool. It does not create a legally effective will, decide that someone has died, or transfer ownership of an account or asset. Legal authority and each provider's own process still matter. Do not enter passwords, complete recovery phrases or credentials that can directly move money.
