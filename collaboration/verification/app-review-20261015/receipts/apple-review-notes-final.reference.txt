Rillora is a private organizer for important accounts, documents, devices and directions. It is not a will, password vault or asset-transfer service. It does not infer death or release information automatically.

REVIEW SIGN-IN
Use the dedicated review username and password supplied in the Sign-In Information fields. Open Profile, tap Sign in, enter the existing email address and select sign in (not sign up). Confirm the displayed account terms when prompted. If passkey enrollment is offered, choose Continue without passkeys; no passkey is required. Accept the standard profile/session access prompt if shown. Google and Apple sign-in are optional alternatives; the supplied review account uses email and password.

CORE FLOW
The review account contains one clearly labelled synthetic App Review example with no personal data. Reviewers may edit this example or add another matter. Add a matter, record a provider/location and directions, then edit it. Profile provides trusted contacts, sharing, Plan & access, and My data. Recipients can also use the web without installing iOS. My data includes export/import and account controls.

SUBSCRIPTION
Free includes one current-updating recipient. Family is annual-only and includes up to five current-updating recipients, shared reviews and one designated contributor whose corrections require the owner to approve them. Open Profile > Plan & access to view the local App Store price and subscribe using Apple Sandbox. Restore purchases and Manage Apple subscriptions are on that page. The review account is enabled for Sandbox; test transactions are kept separate from live customer billing. US annual price: USD119.99. Hong Kong annual price: HKD888.00. The app is free to download.

LANGUAGES
The app supports English and Simplified Chinese. Change the language in Profile settings. English and Chinese store materials are provided separately.
