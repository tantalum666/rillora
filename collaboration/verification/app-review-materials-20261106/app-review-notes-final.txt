Rillora is a private organizer for important digital matters, directions and chosen sharing. It is not a will, password vault, account-transfer service or medical service. It does not infer death or automatically disclose information when someone stops signing in.

SIGN-IN AND CORE FLOW
Use the existing dedicated username/password in Sign-In Information. Open Profile > Sign in; choose sign in, not sign up. Confirm the displayed terms if requested. Passkey enrollment is optional; Continue without passkeys is supported. The supplied account uses email/password and has no enrolled MFA. Google and Apple sign-in are alternatives.

The account contains clearly labelled synthetic planning information, not personal data. Open Matters to add/edit an item and Profile for contacts, sharing, Plan & access and My data. Recipients can use the web without installing iOS; accepting an invitation requires their own account with the invited email verified. My data provides export/import and account controls.

SUBSCRIPTION AND REGIONS
Family is annual-only: up to five live recipients, shared reviews and one designated contributor whose proposed corrections need the planner's approval. Free includes one live recipient. This version and its annual subscription are configured for United States and Hong Kong storefronts. Profile > Plan & access loads the current StoreKit price: USD 119.99/year in the US and HKD 888/year in Hong Kong. Review uses Apple Sandbox; the app is free to download. The supplied account’s previous Sandbox subscription expired on November 6 at 01:01 UTC; its signed expiry event has been reconciled. It is available for the new-subscription path. The monthly SKU remains only for historical restoration, with no new monthly offer. Restore purchases and Manage Apple subscriptions are on the same page. Billing never triggers disclosure.
