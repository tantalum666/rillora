Rillora 1.0 (5) — controlled release candidate

This build adds English and Simplified Chinese throughout the native app, with clearer export labels and improved large-text layouts. It includes the earlier sign-in, recovery and export fixes.

Please check:
• Change Rillora’s language in iOS Settings, relaunch, and confirm saved information is unchanged.
• Review the overview, matters and profile on iPhone/iPad, in portrait/landscape and with larger accessibility text.
• Create and edit a matter, keep or discard an unfinished draft, and follow the next preparation step.
• Invite a recipient, check the exact shared fields, and confirm that stopping sharing removes access.
• Prepare printable directions or an encrypted copy, save the recovery key separately, and verify that leaving the export flow requires an explicit choice.
