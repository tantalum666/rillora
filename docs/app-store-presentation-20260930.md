## Visual and narrative direction

Use the selected paper/forest identity and full03mark,SourceSerif4display type and
Inter body text. Keep large concise headlines,disciplined spacing,illustrative device
frames and quiet paper-like curves. Deep green and pale paper/sage create a coherent
sequence. All copy is rendered from the licensed font outlines,without host-font
substitution. The approved logo geometry remains intact and has no added effects.

The four iPhone stories are:

1. **Leave less to figure out.** A private map of what matters; actual Matters view.
2. **Their view. Your choice.** Selected matters and disclosure level; actual sharing
   view with one magnified crop of its existing Name/Directions/Details control.
3. **A copy to keep close.** Actual audience-bound Start Here preview.
4. **Start with what matters.** Actual public Home and its practical starting point.

Two iPad exports use their own genuine iPad captures,with separate proportions and
an intentional bottom crop that keeps the meaningful app content prominent. No phone
UI is stretched into a tablet. The comparison sheets are supplementary review aids,
not individual images to upload to App Store Connect.

## Source truth and output

Raw screen/brand inputs remain unchanged; SHA256checks and embedded-image checks
confirm their preservation. No source app code,real account data,prices or backend
configuration changes. Private screens contain the existing synthetic household.
Device framing,scaling and the documented detail crop are presentation layers;
there is no invented interface or generated customer testimonial/security badge.

app use and fictional account data. The actual product capabilities and release gates
still determine whether the represented experience is ready to submit.
