## 第一周：从一份文件开始

### 中文

**重要资料放在哪里，家里还有人知道吗？**

不必先整理所有账号。可以从一份保险文件开始，只记三件事：是什么、放在哪里、需要时应该联系谁。

例如：“房屋保险；保单在家庭文件夹；向保险公司了解理赔需要哪些材料。”不用把密码、完整账号或恢复码放进清单。

我是 Rillora 的开发者，正在做一个帮助人们整理重要事务、选择与亲友共享哪些资料的工具。我把起步方法整理成了一份公开清单。你可以先用自己的文档试一条，再决定是否需要软件。

清单：https://www.rillora.xyz/zh-CN/learn/digital-legacy-checklist

### English

**Could someone you trust find one important document?**

Start with one item, not every account you own. Record what it is, where the information is kept, and who to contact for the next step.

For example: “Home insurance; policy in the household folder; ask the insurer about the claim requirements.” Keep passwords and recovery codes out of the list.

I'm building Rillora to help people organize these details and choose what to share with trusted people. I wrote a practical checklist you can try in an ordinary document before deciding whether you need an app.

Checklist: https://www.rillora.xyz/en/learn/digital-legacy-checklist

## 第二周：不同的人看不同的资料

### 中文

**告诉家人一件事，不必交出整份资料。**

想让家人知道保单在哪里，不等于也要共享工作账号、其他订阅或设备信息。

Rillora 可以逐项选择共享的事项和资料范围。对方用自己的账号接受邀请，通过网页也能查看，不需要为了接收资料安装手机应用。

我是开发者，下面的演示只使用虚构事项。判断一次共享是否有用，最简单的方法是：对方只看这一条，能不能知道从哪里开始？

方法：https://www.rillora.xyz/zh-CN/learn/share-digital-legacy-safely

### English

**Sharing one useful detail doesn't require sharing your whole life.**

A person helping with an insurance document may only need its location and the appropriate contact—not unrelated work accounts or subscriptions.

In Rillora, you choose the matters and detail levels a recipient can see. They accept with their own account and can use the web without installing an app.

I'm the developer, and this demonstration uses fictional records. The useful test is simple: can the person understand where to start from the information you chose to share?

Guide: https://www.rillora.xyz/en/learn/share-digital-legacy-safely

## 第三周：已有密码管理器，还缺什么

### 中文

**有了密码管理器，还需要整理什么？**

一组登录信息，未必能说明原始文件由谁保管、哪项订阅需要处理，或你希望家人先联系哪家机构。

Rillora 整理的是这些查找线索和处理意愿。密码与恢复秘密应继续留在专门保护它们的系统里。普通文档也能作为起点；需要按人共享和共同复核时，再考虑相应工具。

我是 Rillora 的开发者。这篇文章说明两类工具各自解决什么问题：https://www.rillora.xyz/zh-CN/learn/password-manager-and-digital-legacy

### English

**A password manager answers one question. Your family may have others.**

Where is the original document? Which subscription should be checked? Who holds the relevant paperwork?

Rillora organizes those location hints and wishes. Sign-in secrets belong in the systems designed to protect them. A document can be a useful starting point; selected sharing and shared reviews solve a different problem.

I'm Rillora's developer. This comparison explains the distinction: https://www.rillora.xyz/en/learn/password-manager-and-digital-legacy

## 第四周：说明撤销能做到什么

### 中文

**共享可以撤销，但已经保存的副本不能凭空收回。**

在 Rillora 中撤销共享，会停止对方通过这项关系继续访问。已经下载、复制或截图的资料，不会因此从对方设备消失。

所以，先决定对方需要哪些资料，再共享，比事后依赖撤销更重要。我是 Rillora 的开发者，也希望把这个边界说明白。

共享前可以核对这份清单：https://www.rillora.xyz/zh-CN/learn/share-digital-legacy-safely

### English

**Revoking access cannot recall a copy someone already saved.**

Revocation in Rillora stops future access through that sharing relationship. It does not erase an earlier download, screenshot or copy on someone else's device.

Choose the information and the recipient before you share. I'm Rillora's developer, and this boundary matters as much as the sharing feature itself.

Before sharing: https://www.rillora.xyz/en/learn/share-digital-legacy-safely
